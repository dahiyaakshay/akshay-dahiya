# Akshay Dahiya

> AI-native SEO and GEO specialist. Building the tools and independent research that measure whether AI systems can actually retrieve, parse and cite a brand, not just rank it.

Seven-plus years across SEO, growth marketing and analytics, now focused on generative engine optimization: how AI search and answer engines crawl, chunk, retrieve and cite web content. Two tools shipped (AI Spider, an AI retrievability audit platform, and MarAI, an AI marketing operations platform), two more in development, and an independent whitepaper series (the GEO Retrieval Research Series) currently four papers in.
