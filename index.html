Independent research on GEO, AI citation behavior, and retrieval systems — measured across thousands of prompts, not assumed.

001: Crawl Freshness vs AI Visibility

A four-month benchmark tracking how quickly updated pages begin appearing in AI-generated answers across seven AI search and answer engines.

Problem

Marketers assumed fresh content meant fast AI citation, but the actual delay had never been measured.

Process

Tracked 400 pages across seven AI platforms daily for four months, monitoring first-citation latency and authority signals.

Solution

Built the first cross-platform benchmark linking crawl freshness, domain authority, and AI citation speed.

Result

  • 14 day median first citation
  • 34% citation replacement rate

002: Multi-Hop Citation Paths in AI Retrieval

14,000 prompts across seven AI platforms reveal how often AI systems combine information from multiple web pages before answering.

Problem

No one had measured how often AI systems synthesize multiple sources rather than citing a single page.

Process

Ran 14,000 prompts across seven platforms, tracing citation hops, fan-out, and evidence chains.

Solution

Introduced six new metrics to quantify multi-hop retrieval depth across platforms and industries.

Result

  • 45.2% multi-hop citation rate
  • 82% rate for complex queries

003: What AI Actually Reads on Your Webpage

A live-environment testing methodology traced exactly which sections of 45,000 webpages AI systems actually surfaced before answering.

Problem

Assumed AI reads full pages, but retrieval behavior at the chunk level had never been measured.

Process

Ran 2,500 queries natively in live LLM environments to trace section-by-section retrieval across 45,000 pages and six platforms.

Solution

Mapped section-level retrieval probability, revealing a clear top-heavy retrieval gradient.

Result

  • 12 to 45% of a page retrieved
  • 88% introduction retrieval rate
