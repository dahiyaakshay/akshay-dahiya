About

Seven-plus years in performance marketing, now spent building what measures whether AI can find you at all.

Growth and SEO specialist by background, AI-native MarTech builder by current focus. The tooling and research here measure AI retrievability directly, rather than just writing about it.

Expertise

Both sides of search: the traditional discipline and the emerging one.

On one side, the traditional discipline: technical, on-page, off-page and e-commerce SEO, paid media across Google, Bing and Meta, conversion optimisation, and the analytics stack (GA4, GTM, attribution and funnel reporting) that ties it to revenue. On the other, the emerging one: AI retrievability auditing, citation tracking across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity, and the entity and knowledge-graph work that underpins generative engine optimisation. Seven-plus years cover both, with the balance shifting steadily toward the second.
